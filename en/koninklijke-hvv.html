The club and the venue

Koninklijke HVV

The VAC is played at the Koninklijke Haagsche Cricket & Voetbal Vereeniging, known in The Hague simply as HVV. A club with a long football history, and the home of the VAC on Friday evenings.

History

Before the First World War, HVV was the most successful football club in the Netherlands, winning ten national titles between 1890 and 1914. In 1978 the club was granted the title Koninklijke (Royal).

Since 1898 the club has played at De Diepput, on the border of Benoordenhout and Wassenaar. That is also where the VAC teams take the field on Friday evenings.
